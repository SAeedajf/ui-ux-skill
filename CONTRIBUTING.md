# Contribution and evidence policy

Keep Skills focused on a user task. Add guidance only when it changes a decision or helps verify an outcome; avoid growing the repository with generic checklists.

When adding a source, record its stable ID, title, canonical URL, source class, evidence type, access status, review date, and concise notes. Use two independent review passes and checkpoint every five sources. Mark inaccessible, gated, stale, or ambiguous material as partial; do not infer the contents of a book or article from its title. Prefer primary standards and official platform documentation for normative or implementation claims.

Write original summaries. Do not copy substantial prose, screenshots, diagrams, code, or assets from references. Keep each recommendation scoped to the population, task, platform, and conditions supported by its evidence. Distinguish observed user research from expert opinion and visual inspiration.

Evaluation cases are specifications until run. Record model/host, date, prompt, result, and reviewer notes before changing a case to passed. Avoid storing private user data in examples or evals.
